## Key question 1 — Which Samsung Electronics patents (US and KR) since 2014 claim statistical or typical-corner timing signoff, statistical corner libraries, OCV/POCV/LVF derate generation, or global/local variation modeling for STA? Confirm US 9,977,845 (inventors, KR priority, claim 1)

### Takeaway
US 9,977,845 B2 is confirmed as a Samsung STA patent claiming priority to KR 10-2015-0010681 (filed 22 Jan 2015) whose independent claim loads a library containing local random variation information plus global variation information derived from a set of global variation parameters, computes arc delays, and decides timing violations from the delays, the local random variation and the global variation, with slack computed from a statistical (root-sum-square) sum of the path's local random variation value and global variation value; global variation values are characterized at the SS and FF corners, divided by 3 to a 1σ scale, and can be evaluated at corners not present in the library. A second Samsung family, US 10,192,015 / KR 2016-0147435 A, groups STA critical paths by "criticality sigma level" (GBA extraction, PBA at each group's maximum sigma) to estimate yield and drive optimization. No other Samsung post-2014 US/KR patent on OCV/POCV/LVF derate generation or statistical corners surfaced in 14 English and Korean queries; inventors and exact dates of both families could not be retrieved.

### Inferences
- The claim structure of US 9,977,845 (library = local-random σ information + global-variation values normalized to 1σ from SS/FF corner characterization; slack = nominal delays ± RSS(local, global)) is exactly a "nominal/typical timing plus combined global+local statistical margin" signoff, i.e. the closest patent match to topic (a); the "corners not included in the library" language indicates interpolation/extrapolation of the global term across corners, i.e. a global-corner-dependent margin (topic (d) in a mild form).
- US 10,192,015 presupposes a σ-parameterized STA (POCV-style criticality sigma per path) and turns it into a yield metric — evidence that Samsung's signoff flow by 2015–2016 was statistical at the path level.
- Given KR 10-2015-0010681, the KR publication of US 9,977,845 should exist as a KR 10-2016-00xxxxx A (≈ Aug 2016) with a title close to "집적 회로의 정적 타이밍 분석 방법"; this must be confirmed on KIPRIS.

### Inferences
- US 8,707,230's explicit "global corner vs total corner vs local variation" picture (local ellipses around each global corner, a larger total-variation envelope) is the same conceptual model as the "total variation corner"/"global corner + local σ" split asked about in topic (b), but the patent applies it to Monte-Carlo parasitic/coupling simulation, not to STA signoff margins.
- US 8,972,919 is a deterministic, corner-style treatment of a systematic (chip-wide) misalignment variable in which launch and capture paths get opposite extremes — the opposite of "common-mode credit" (topic (e)); it is useful to the writer as evidence of how TSMC handled a global-type variation on clock vs data paths in 2013–2015.

## Key question 4 — Confirm the assignee and content of US 11,893,332 ("Global mistracking analysis in integrated circuit design") and related "mistracking" patents
