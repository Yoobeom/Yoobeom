## Key Question 1 — Which conference/journal papers compare "SS corner + OCV/AOCV" vs "corner + POCV/LVF" vs "statistical / typical-corner signoff" on real designs, and what do the known leads (Kahng DAC 2015, Tetelbaum 2014, J. Phys. Conf. Ser. 1706, UCSD corner-reduction work) actually contain?

### Takeaway
No peer-reviewed paper was found that reports a side-by-side, same-design comparison of SS+OCV vs SS+POCV/LVF vs TT-corner+statistical-margin signoff with frequency/area/power numbers; the closest public items are (i) practitioner/industry papers that qualitatively trace OCV→AOCV→POCV/LVF and report per-model PrimeTime slack on one design (*J. Phys.: Conf. Ser.* 1706, 2020), (ii) Tetelbaum's 2014 white papers that quantify corner explosion (12–126 corners in 2013, predicted 16–248 by 2020) and argue for statistical signoff, and (iii) a growing corner-reduction literature (Kahng et al. DATE 2019; One-for-All ICCAD 2024; FACT TODAES 2025/26) that reduces the *number of observed corners* by ML rather than by replacing corners with a statistical global margin. Kahng's DAC 2015 history paper confirms "variation modeling and signoff corner definition, including variation-aware path-based STA" as a central theme but its numbers could not be extracted (PDF blocked).

### Inferences
- The production literature treats "global vs local" exactly as the TT-signoff argument requires — global (die-to-die) variation handled by *corners*, local by POCV/SOCV/LVF sigma within one run (Cadence Signoff Summit 2013; EDN/patent snippet; SemiWiki CLKDA description of SSG/TTG/FFG global corners with local MC parameters — see KQ2). What is *not* found in any paper is the further step of replacing the SS/FF *global* corner by a global-sigma term added statistically to the local sigma at TT; the industry response to corner explosion documented here is corner *selection/prediction* (Abelite "16 corners are enough", Kahng DATE 2019, One-for-All, FACT), not corner *elimination* by a statistical global margin.
- The RISC-V ASIC characterization (arXiv 2512.13866) gives the only public per-design decomposition in this pass: ~12% Fmax mean shift TT→SS (1.85→1.63 GHz) versus 9–17 ps per-stage slack sigma from LVF; this is the kind of number a TT+statistical-margin proposal would need to justify RSS-combining global and local terms.
- Corner counts cited across sources are consistent in order of magnitude: 16 (40 nm, 2011), 12–126 (2013), 58 (16 nm block, 2019), "hundreds" (FACT, 2024–25), 800–1,100 "corner combinations" (2018 trade press, likely counting mode×corner views).

**PrimeTime POCV semantics relevant to a "TT + N-sigma" methodology** `[LOCAL-DOC PT UG Q-2019.12]`
- POCV "models the timing behavior of each instance as a function of a variable specific to that instance"; it provides "Statistical single-parameter derating for random variations," "Limited statistical reporting (mean, sigma) for timing paths," "Reduced pessimism gap between graph-based analysis and path-based analysis"; requires a PrimeTime-ADV license.
- "PrimeTime commands report 'corner' values computed on the right side (for maximum values) or left side (for minimum values) of the distributions. Constraint values are always computed on the right side because higher values are more constraining for both setup and hold. By default, corner values are computed at 3 sigma."
- Delay-calculation report equations (exactly as printed):
  `Cell delay derated = "Cell delay" * ( "POCVM guardband" * "POCVM distance derate" + "Incremental derate" )`
  `Cell delay sigma = "POCVM delay sigma" * ( "POCVM guardband" * "POCVM coef scale factor" )`
  with the printed example `POCVM delay sigma = 0.000652 0.000500`, `POCVM guardband = 0.980000`, `Cell delay derated = 0.012780 0.009804`, `Cell delay sigma = 0.000639 0.000490`; with via variation enabled the report adds "POCVM delay sigma induced by wire variation" and "POCVM output transition sigma induced by wire variation."
- Derate example in the UG: "`set_timing_derate -early 0.95 -late 1.05 -pocvm_guardband`" (# add global margin to entire distribution (mean and standard deviation)) and "`set_timing_derate -early 1.03 -late 1.03 -pocvm_coefficient_scale_factor [get_cells {BLK1 BLK2}]`" (# add block-specific variation margin (standard deviation only)). A plain `set_timing_derate` on an instance *overrides* its POCV model ("the arc contributes a derated nominal scalar delay value instead of a variation distribution").
- Statistical attributes: `max_fall_variation_slack.mean` / `.std_dev` etc. ("POCV slack and arrival attributes are statistical quantities; they have mean and standard deviation"); "Statistical Graph Merging Pessimism" is removed by the tool when statistical maxima are taken at multi-input gates.
- Other verified POCV variables (Vars W-2024.09-SP3): `timing_pocvm_enable_analysis` (default false; "Unlike in AOCV mode, there is no mode where POCV can be used in PBA only"; auto-switches to on_chip_variation), `timing_enable_slew_variation` (default true), `timing_enable_constraint_variation` (default false), `timing_pocvm_max_transition_sigma` / `_clock_sigma` / `_sequential_sigma`, `timing_pocvm_enable_extended_moments` (default false; "The extended moments are mean_shift, std_dev and skewness as defined in Liberty LVF extension in order to model non Gaussian distribution"), `timing_pocvm_enable_delay_slew_correlation` (default false), `timing_enable_via_variation`, `timing_pocvm_precedence`, `timing_crpr_different_transition_variation_derate`, `timing_use_slew_variation_in_constraint_arcs`.

### Inferences
- Both timers expose exactly one statistical knob per view — the N-sigma at which the (local-variation) slack distribution is evaluated — plus multiplicative guardbands on mean/sigma. A "TT + statistical global margin" flow therefore has to be *composed by the user* (e.g., raising `timing_pocvm_corner_sigma`/`set_socv_reporting_nsigma_multiplier`, or applying `-pocvm_guardband`/`-pocvm_coefficient_scale_factor` derived from a global sigma), since neither tool has a native global-variation term. - The reference-methodology example (`-pocvm_guardband -early 0.97 … -corner corner1/corner2`) shows the intended production usage: guardbands set *per corner* on top of LVF — i.e., corners are retained.

### Inferences
- The corpus confirms that "corner reduction" in production practice (2011–2025) means selecting/predicting fewer PVT×RC corners, and that "statistical corner" in tool vocabulary means the N-sigma point of the local-variation distribution (`timing_pocvm_corner_sigma`, `set_socv_reporting_nsigma_multiplier`), not a statistically derived replacement for the SS/FF global corner.
