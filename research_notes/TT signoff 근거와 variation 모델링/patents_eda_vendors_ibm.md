# Patents by EDA vendors and IBM on statistical timing with global/local variation, POCV/SOCV/LVF, statistical corners, and typical-corner / statistical-corner signoff

Verification method and its limits (read first)
- patents.google.com, USPTO PPUBS, Justia, FreePatentsOnline, Espacenet, Lens and every other patent HTML site were blocked by the sandbox egress proxy. The only reachable primary source was Google's patent-image store, which serves the official USPTO PDF of each document at `https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/pdfs/US<number>.pdf` (grants) or `.../pdfs/US<year><7-digit>.pdf` (pre-grant publications). Every patent marked "VERIFIED" below was downloaded from that store and its front page (title, inventors, assignee, application number, filing date, prior-publication number, related-application data, grant date), abstract and, where the OCR text layer allowed, claim 1 were read from the PDF. Google-Patents URLs are given for convenience (they are deterministic: `https://patents.google.com/patent/<number><kind>/en`) but were NOT fetched.
- The legacy PDF path only exists for documents up to roughly early 2015 (US 8,95x,xxx / US 2015/00xxxxx). Every candidate granted or published after that (US 9.x-11.x million, 2015+ publications) could not be verified beyond search-engine snippets and is listed separately as "PARTIALLY VERIFIED (search snippet only)".
- Foreign family members (KR/CN/JP/EP/TW) are not printed on US front pages; the only family data available are US continuations/divisionals/provisionals and PCT numbers printed on the US document. Foreign families are therefore a gap for all entries.
- Several PDFs have an incomplete OCR text layer (claims pages blank); for those, claim 1 is marked "not extractable" and the abstract/description is used instead.
- The web-search budget was exhausted after 200 queries, so candidate discovery relied on (a) the search results obtained before that and (b) a citation "snowball" over the References-Cited sections of every downloaded PDF (about 350 documents examined in total).

## Master annotated list: which patents were verified, who holds them, and what claim 1 covers (by assignee, with timeline)

### Takeaway
About 60 relevant US documents were verified from the official PDFs. IBM holds by far the deepest portfolio on canonical-form SSTA and on projecting/hybridizing statistical timing with corners (2002-2014 filings); Synopsys' own filings cover mismatch-based variation-aware library generation (2008) and the SCS-OCV/POCV "N-sigma statistical corner" patents (priority Aug 2010), and Synopsys also now owns Magma's "statistical OCV" margin patents (2008) via the 2012 Magma acquisition; Cadence's verified holdings are the Kriplani confidence-level SSTA family (priority Oct 2004), the Kariat/Phillips/Keller sensitivity-based parameterized timing patent (priority Nov 2005), the Goyal variation-aware ETM patent (priority Aug 2007) and the design-specific OCV (DS-OCV) derate patent (priority Dec 2009). No patent assigned to Altos Design Automation on statistical (Variety) characterization was found; Solido and CLK DA each have one verified pre-2015 patent; several very relevant "statistical corner"/global-local separation patents belong to non-EDA companies (TSMC, Freescale, TI, LSI, Agere).

## Which patents claim the "canonical form" statistical timing model with global and independent random terms, and which claim projection to corners / sensitivity-based corner analysis / parametric yield / criticality?

### Takeaway
IBM's Sep. 2003 trio (US 7,428,716 and its continuation US 8,010,921; US 7,111,260 and divisional US 7,512,919; US 7,086,023) are the canonical-form patents: their descriptions define delay = a0 + Σ ai·Δxi + a(n+1)·ΔR with a "correlated (or global) portion" and an "independent (or local) portion", while their claims recite statistical arrival times as "a weighted sum of probability distributions of ... sources of variation". Corner projection is claimed most directly by US 8,141,012 (single SSTA run, project to k selected corners, close timing on worst corners) and US 2012/0117527 (non-separable statistical + corner-based parameters, single run, projection to corner values); sensitivity-based hybrid corner analysis by US 7,555,740 / 2008/0209372 / 7,886,246; criticality by US 7,086,023, 7,437,697, 7,861,199 and (Extreme DA) 7,487,486; parametric yield by US 7,480,880 / 7,861,199 / 2011/0106497.
